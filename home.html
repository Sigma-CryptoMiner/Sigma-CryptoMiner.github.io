Welcome to the Repentance Plus Mod Wiki!
The official wiki for the Repentance Plus Mod. This mod was designed to provide a more thorough Repentance experience, bringing all remaining unlocks thematically fitting for Tainted Characters into the game. Currently (as of version 1.36), this mod provides a ton of additional content for The Binding of Isaac: Repentance, including:
  • 59 items
  • 23 trinkets
  • 41 cards and runes and 6 pill effects
  • 4 new chests with unique mechanics
  • 17 new heart types resembling Tainted characters
  • A new 'tinted' alternative of spiked rocks with a valuable loot
  • A very cute beggar!
  • And a bit more?
